# Phase L1 — data provenance

L1 adds **no runtime data**. The rendered set is still the 273 fixtures of the Step 6 pilot and
Phases A1–A3 (`src/lib/data/streetlights*.json`, unchanged).

How each fixture is made (all estimates):
- Position: the L0 centreline station moved perpendicular to the nearest rendered segment of the same
  way by half its rendered width + 0.8 m (brackets) / + 0.6 m (lanterns), always to the right of the
  way direction (24 841 fixtures). 200 stations on ways absent from the rendered map stay on the
  centreline; brackets there take their orientation from the L0 way geometry.
- Orientation: brackets aim back at the road; lanterns have none.
- Photometry, height, range, colour temperature: the Step 6 type table.
- Occlusion: 16 sectors × 3 rays against building footprints of the rendered map, from the emitter,
  like the corridor generator; bridge decks, water, rails, platforms and seams are not considered.

## Coordinates

Stress-set positions are scene units (`x = (E − 569030) / (25 × 0.9996584126)`,
`z = (6494989 − N) / (25 × 0.9996584126)`, x east, z south), converted from the EPSG:3006 L0 stations
with `src/lib/geo/sweref99tm.js`; the registry stores E/N recomputed from the scene values
(consistency checked by the registry validator). Heights come from the rendered ground at load time
(RH2000-based DTM, 1 scene unit = 25 m). No vertical data is stored.

## Measurements

Performance and capture data in [data/](data/) are measurements on one machine (AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D,
RTX 5070 Ti, Edge 156 / ANGLE D3D11, Windows 11), not data about Norrköping. Raw captures and runs stay
in ignored `work/lighting-city-l1/`.
